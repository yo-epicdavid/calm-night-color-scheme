# Calm Night

A **low-eye-strain** dark theme, tuned against the research on
_screen fatigue_. See [the writeup](../docs/RESEARCH.md).

## Principles

1. Don't max out contrast — avoid halation
2. Desaturate — reduce chromatic aberration
3. Cap the color count — lower cognitive load

> Reserve color for what matters: strings, magnitudes, comments.

    const accent = "#f0c674"; // the one warm accent
